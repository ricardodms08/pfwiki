# Clearance Delivery
- MACTR (Tower) is responsible for validating new flight plans from LPMA, issuing enroute clearances and takes part in managing and enforcing the departure sequence.
- DCL system is not available at Madeira, therefore, clearances must be obtained via voice, not PDC.
- All procedures, except GALOZ and NIDUL, initial climb FL60. GALOZ and NIDUL initial climb FL100.
- VFR Circuits are permitted. However, they are highly discouraged as they create conflicts with arriving IFR traffic.
- VFR departures that intend to leave the CTR must always be coordinated with the position directly above.

# Phraseology
## IFR Clearance
- "[Callsign], information [ATIS Letter], QNH [QNH], cleared to [destination], [SID] departure, climb [initial climb], squawk [code]."
- "[Callsign], readback correct, report ready."
- "[Callsign], negative, [correction]."
## VFR Clearance
- **Circuits**: "[Callsign], information [ATIS Letter], QNH [QNH], cleared from Madeira to Madeira, after departure join [circuit leg], climb to [altitude], squawk [code]."
- **Cross-Country**: "[Callsign], information [ATIS Letter], QNH [QNH], cleared from Madeira to [destination], after departure proceed to [visual reference point], climb to [altitude], squawk [code]."
## Frequencies
- 118.350 MHz (LPMA_TWR) - one one eight **decimal** three five zero
- 119.605 MHz (LPMA_APP) - one one niner **decimal** six zero five
- 132.255 MHz (LPPC_I_CTR) - one three two **decimal** two five five
- 118.100 MHz - one one eight **decimal** one
## Altimeter Setting
- QNH 1009 - "QNH one zero zero niner"
- QNH 1000 - "QNH one thousand"
- QNH 993 - "QNH niner niner three"

## Altitude
- Levels such as FL100, FL200, FL300 are said "One Hundred / Two Hundred / Three Hundred".
